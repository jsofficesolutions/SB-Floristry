## A Tribute in Living Flora

Created to celebrate the extraordinary life of an English architect, this commission reflected a profound love of natural geometry, white flowers, and winter fragrance.

## Execution & Funeral Liaison

- **Full Direct Coordination:** Our founders liaised directly with the funeral director and family, eliminating administrative burdens during mourning.
- **The Casket Masterpiece:** Cascading English white delphiniums, avalanche roses, winter snowberries, and feathery cedar, composed entirely without plastics or floral foam.
- **Chapel Floral Columns:** Twin symmetrical pedestals framing the altar, offering gentle botanical calm and natural solace to gathered mourners.

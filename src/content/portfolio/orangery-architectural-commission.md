## The Private Commission

Bespoke residential installations represent the purest expression of our founders' architectural floristry. Created for an exclusive summer weekend celebration, this piece acted as an ephemeral companion to monumental stone sculpture.

## Botanical Craft

- **Harmonising with Heritage Stone:** Incorporating blackened urns and patinated bronze vessels that echoed the gallery's 18th-century stonework.
- **Sculptural Balance:** High-contrast floral accents against sprawling wild branches, balancing negative space and natural movement.
- **Longevity & Care:** Direct founders' maintenance across the celebratory weekend, ensuring every bloom remained in peak form.

## The Vision & Context

Conceived for an intimate multi-day summer gathering, this wedding commission required transforming a historic Georgian manor into an ethereal sensory journey. The brief called for botanical restraint: honoring classical architecture while bringing wild English estate gardens into monolithic stone salons.

## Floral Architecture & Spatial Design

Our founders designed a sequential transition through the estate:

- **The Ceremony Archway:** Constructed upon an invisible bronze framework, blooming with garden roses, British delphiniums, and rambling sweet peas that mirrored the natural estate trellises.
- **The Central Hall Monoliths:** Symmetrical 8-foot stone urns framing the grand stairwell, designed to scent the arrival with night-blooming jasmine and heirloom garden foliage.
- **The Dining Tablescape:** Organic silk runners adorned with hand-carved alabaster vessels, taper candles, and individual stem clusters allowing uninterrupted sightlines across the long banquet table.

Every bloom was hand-harvested within 48 hours of the celebration, ensuring unblemished petals that opened precisely at twilight.

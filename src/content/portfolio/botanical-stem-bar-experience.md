## The Concept

The Stem Bar reimagines wedding favours and party tokens as an experiential art station. Guests are invited to converse with our studio florists, discovering rare seasonal stems, unusual foliage textures, and historic flower varieties.

## The Guest Experience

- **The Curation Station:** Over thirty bespoke flower buckets in brushed brass and blackened stoneware, filled with fragrant sweet peas, ranunculus, and garden spray roses.
- **Master Florist Crafting:** Each arrangement was composed live to the guest's personal preference, finished with trailing raw silk ribbon and embossed personalized wax seals.
- **Sensory Memorabilia:** Guests departed with an indelible living memory of the celebration, accompanied by custom botanical care guides.

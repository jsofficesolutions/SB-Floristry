## The Occasion

Commissioned for an international heritage maison hosting an annual founders' dinner, this celebration demanded intense visual drama under candlelit chandeliers. The concept embraced quiet darkness punctuated by warm metallic embers and sculptural stems.

## Spatial Orchestration

- **The Foyer Arrival:** Sculptural smoked glass vessels bearing oversized branches of blackened beech and Japanese maple.
- **The Banqueting Tablescape:** Over forty metres of continuous botanical composition featuring black baccara roses, burgundy calla lilies, and antique dahlias interspersed with custom obsidian candlesticks.
- **Sensory Cohesion:** Scent-mapped arrangements ensuring the natural floral fragrance enhanced the bespoke multi-course menu rather than competing with it.
